## Core flow — "One tap, three things happen"
1. Tap what you need — pick from your catalogue (coffee, prints, IT help…), add a note, tap Buzz.
   Choose a destination (e.g. "Boss Cabin").
2. Right team gets pinged — pantry team, print room or IT desk notified at once: app, Telegram,
   WhatsApp, email. Notifications repeat till accepted.
3. First to accept owns it — whoever's free taps Accept. Requester sees who's on it (name + photo),
   ETA, and rates them when done.
Lifecycle states seen in product: Buzzed/requested → Accepted → Started (with ETA) → Delivered
(photo can be attached) → Rated (1–5★). Every request is timed.

## Feature list (from site)
- Pantry catalogue: coffee, tea, juice, snacks, dry fruits — usual order one tap away.
- Print room: upload PDF, set copies & colour, prints delivered to your seat/room.
- IT & facilities: AC too cold, projector stuck, HDMI needed — auto-routes to the right team.
- Courier pickup: reception/mailroom logs pickups; audit-trailed.
- Multi-channel: App + Telegram + WhatsApp + email at the same time. Repeats till accepted.
- First-accept wins: sent to the whole team; first to tap Accept owns it. No "I thought you'd do it".
- SLA & escalation: every request has a deadline; overdue ones auto-escalate to a manager
  (escalation chain on Pro).
- Analytics + scorecard: who's fastest, who gets 5★, when the office buzzes most.
- Roles & audit log: granular permissions per role; every action audit-logged; owner-only spend view.
- Request cost visibility: owner sees the cost (e.g. of a lunch order).
- Mobile app: rings through even on silent or a locked phone; one tap to summon staff or security,
  raise an emergency, or place an order; accept & track requests on the move.
  Android APK, v1.16.0 (build 48), 61 MB. Account & workspace live on the server.
