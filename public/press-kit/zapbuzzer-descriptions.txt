NAME
ZapBuzzer (one word, capital Z and capital B). The app itself is often called "Buzzer".

TAGLINE
Press a button. Staff knows.

ONE LINE
ZapBuzzer is the internal-request CRM for offices that hate phone tag.

SHORT (about 50 words)
ZapBuzzer is an internal-request CRM for offices. Employees tap what they need — coffee,
prints, IT help, facilities or a courier pickup — and the right team is notified on the app,
Telegram, WhatsApp and email until someone accepts. The first person to accept owns the
request. Every request is timed and rated.

LONG (about 130 words)
ZapBuzzer is the internal-request CRM your office should have had years ago. It was founded
in a Pune office tired of phone tag: three calls for one coffee, print jobs lost in a WhatsApp
group, IT tickets dying in someone's DMs.

With ZapBuzzer, an employee picks a request from the office catalogue, adds a note and taps
Buzz. The pantry, print room, IT desk, facilities or reception team is pinged on every channel
until someone accepts. The first to accept owns it; the requester sees who is on it and an ETA,
and rates the work when it is delivered. SLA timers auto-escalate overdue requests, and
analytics and scorecards give support staff fair credit.

ZapBuzzer runs on the web and on Android, with Free, Pro and Enterprise plans.
